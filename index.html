Can you trust it?

We rated players using only past seasons, then checked against what they actually did next season (2023 to 2026). The score is a correlation: 0 means no better than a coin toss. Bowling under pressure is a repeatable skill and the rating finds it; batting under pressure is close to random for every method, so read the batting list as "best bets", not certainties.

How to read this
High-pressure ballThe quarter of all balls where the next delivery could move the win probability the most. A tight chase in over 17 counts; a dead rubber's death overs do not.
RatingRuns added (batters) or saved (bowlers) per 100 high-pressure IPL balls, compared with what that exact situation usually yields. Wickets count as runs saved.
Usual levelThe same measure on every ball he has played, IPL and T20I. A short hot streak is pulled toward this, so luck can't top the list.
90% rangeWhere the true rating probably sits. When two players' ranges overlap, their order could flip.
Top-10 oddsHow often he stays in the top 10 when we replay match history 300 times with random variations.
Eligibility100+ IPL pressure balls since 2022, 60+ in 2025-26, played IPL 2026 and performed at or above par in it.
